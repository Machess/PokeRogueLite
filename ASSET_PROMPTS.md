# Generated regional artwork

Mode: built-in image generation, new images without references, opaque backgrounds. Generated PNGs are included unchanged in `assets/regions/`; labels, route overlays and the player marker are rendered separately by `js/region-map.js`. Geography is stylized. The coordinate overlays were aligned to the resulting art rather than assuming every requested location was reproduced exactly.

## assets/regions/kanto.png

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: in-game illustrated KANTO region map background, landscape 3:2. Crisp beautiful 16-bit pixel art cartography fitting a Pokémon handheld game, top-down overhead, readable large terrain masses, no perspective tilt. This is Kanto: green mainland with ocean south and east, rugged mountains along north and northwest, western forest, central plains, southeastern safari forest, southwestern volcanic island and small southern sea islands. Landmark positions as fractions of canvas: Pallet tiny coastal village at (0.24,0.68), Viridian at (0.24,0.48), Pewter at (0.24,0.24), Cerulean at (0.62,0.22), Vermilion harbor at (0.64,0.61), Celadon at (0.44,0.43), Saffron at (0.63,0.43), Lavender at (0.84,0.43), Fuchsia at (0.66,0.78), Cinnabar volcanic island at (0.24,0.88), Indigo plateau stone stadium among northwest mountains at (0.10,0.16). Small orange and red rooftops mark towns at exactly those positions. Pale pathways connect mainland towns; water-route dashes over sea. Carefully detailed tile forests, cliff terraces, beaches and wave pixels. Soft grassy green, turquoise water, ochre trails, warm roofs. No labels, NO text, no typography, no logos, no characters, no pins, no interface, no legend, no border; software will overlay precise city names and a moving player marker. Entire region visible with narrow ocean margin.
